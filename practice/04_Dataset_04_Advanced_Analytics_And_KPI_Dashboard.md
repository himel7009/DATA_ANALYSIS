# 🎯 Practice Problem Set 04: Customer Lifecycle, Cohorts & Executive KPI Analytics
**Dataset**: `Dataset_04.csv`  
**Scenario**: You are a Lead Strategic Business Analyst reporting directly to the Chief Operations Officer (COO) and Chief Marketing Officer (CMO). Leadership requires advanced analytics to identify high-value customer clusters, diagnose customer retention over time, audit shipping SLA compliance, uncover cross-selling product bundles, and synthesize the business's key performance indicators into a unified executive visual dashboard.

---

### Part 1: Pipeline Initialization & Data Preparation
1. Load the transactional database and ensure numeric fields (especially revenue figures with commas) and dates are correctly parsed.
2. Establish derived operational attributes, including the exact fulfillment duration in days.
3. Validate data integrity by ensuring there are no duplicate order entries or records with corrupt timestamps.

---

### Part 2: RFM Customer Segmentation Model
4. Establish an analysis anchor date set immediately after the latest recorded transaction in the dataset.
5. For every unique customer in the database, calculate their core RFM metrics:
   * **Recency**: The number of days elapsed since the customer's most recent purchase.
   * **Frequency**: The total count of distinct purchase orders placed by the customer.
   * **Monetary**: The total cumulative dollar revenue generated by the customer.
6. Evaluate the statistical distribution of the RFM metrics: determine the median, average, and interquartile spread for Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.
7. Discretize customer Recency into four scoring tiers (scores 1 to 4), ensuring that customers who purchased recently receive the highest score.
8. Discretize customer Frequency and Monetary values into four scoring tiers (scores 1 to 4), ensuring that high-volume and high-spending customers receive the highest score.
9. Synthesize the individual scores into strategic customer segments:
   * **Champions**: Customers who score at the highest levels across Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.
   * **Loyal Customers**: Frequent buyers who purchase regularly.
   * **At-Risk Customers**: Previously frequent buyers who have not returned recently.
   * **New / Occasional Buyers**: Recent purchasers with low transaction frequency.
   * **Lost / Churned Customers**: Infrequent buyers who have been inactive for an extended period.
10. Calculate the headcount and the percentage share of the customer base represented by each strategic segment.
11. Calculate the total revenue and the percentage of company-wide revenue contributed by each segment. Does a small group of Champions drive an outsized share of revenue?

---

### Part 3: Cohort Retention Analysis (Customer Lifecycle Tracking)
12. Assign each transaction to its calendar order period (Year-Month).
13. Determine each customer's acquisition cohort: the specific month when they placed their very first order on the platform.
14. Calculate the cohort lifecycle index for every purchase: the exact number of months that elapsed between the customer's acquisition month and the month of the subsequent transaction.
15. Aggregate the data into a cohort tracking matrix showing the number of unique active customers returning in each elapsed month for every acquisition cohort.
16. Convert the raw customer counts into a **Percentage Retention Matrix**, calculating the proportion of the original cohort that returned to purchase in Month 1, Month 2, Month 3, and so on up to Month 12+.
17. Identify which historical customer acquisition cohorts demonstrate the strongest retention rates over time.
18. Measure the average 12-month retention rate across all cohorts: what percentage of acquired customers are still purchasing after one year?

---

### Part 4: Supply Chain Logistics & SLA Performance
19. Analyze order dispatch performance: calculate the average fulfillment lead time across each order priority classification (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
20. Check whether the operational team actually fulfills Critical orders faster than Low-priority orders.
21. Define business fulfillment Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for each shipping tier:
   * Same Day: Dispatch on Day 0
   * First Class: Dispatch within 2 days
   * Second Class: Dispatch within 4 days
   * Standard Class: Dispatch within 6 days
22. Audit every transaction against its SLA benchmark and flag all orders that failed to meet their promised delivery schedule.
23. Calculate the global SLA violation rate: what percentage of customer orders suffered from delayed dispatch?
24. Identify which global markets and shipping tiers suffer from the highest rate of delayed orders.
25. Calculate the freight burden: compute the average shipping cost incurred per unit sold across each product category.

---

### Part 5: Market Basket & Cross-Selling Patterns
26. Filter the database to isolate multi-item orders (orders containing more than one distinct product line).
27. Calculate the proportion of total orders that represent multi-item transactions versus single-item purchases.
28. For multi-item transactions, determine which product sub-categories are most frequently co-purchased together within the same order.
29. How can the merchandising and marketing teams leverage these co-occurrence pairs to design bundle promotions or automated checkout recommendation widgets?
30. Determine the company's overall customer repeat rate: what percentage of all recorded customers have placed more than one order throughout their relationship with the business?

---

### Part 6: Executive KPI Visual Dashboard
31. Construct a visual representation of customer segments: plot Recency against Monetary spend on a scatter plot, using color coding to distinguish between the strategic customer tiers.
32. Display the monthly Cohort Retention Matrix as a formatted triangular heatmap, displaying retention percentages clearly across each cohort lifecycle month.
33. Create an operational bar chart illustrating the shipping SLA breach rate across the different global markets.
34. Design and build a unified 4-panel Executive Dashboard on a single layout canvas:
   * Panel 1: Revenue contribution by customer segment.
   * Panel 2: Monthly active unique customer trajectory over time.
   * Panel 3: Order value frequency distribution.
   * Panel 4: Average fulfillment duration across shipping service tiers.
